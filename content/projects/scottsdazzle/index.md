## Project Overview

The City of Scottsdale was looking for a website that their marketing team could promote a number of holiday-themed events throughout Old Town Scottsdale. It would need to be accessible for public use and backend-friendly for daily updating by their team. WordPress to the rescue! Combined with Advanced Custom Fields for unified entry fields, the shops, restaurants and events remained streamlined and easily updated.

The website needed to feature events (primarily) and vendor offerings based on the Scottsdazzle theme. Local shops, art galleries, restaurants, and others were encouraged to submit happenings or seasonal deals using "Scottsdazzle" promoition codes. In turn, they would be featured on the website free of charge. This drove 100,000+ link clicks, 1.7 million video views, 22.8 million impressions, and a total of 300,000+ page views in 2025. The site was a success.

### Come one, come all

#half-description
The events were the hub of the site. Vendors were able to submit their events to a Gravity Form to automatically post to the site. The Scottsdazzle marketing team could then review the submission and publish when they were ready. The process was minimal with just the right mount of control for their team. Custom categories to manage each event status (sold out, cancelled, passed, etc) made display notifications easy to use as well.
