## Project Overview

Sanders + Parks is a perfect testament to quality design. Launched in early 2019, it still looks and functions in a modern and relevant experience. Their leadership and IT team wanted something different than the traditional law firm website. The site needed to be something that they could manage internally (unlike some law firm web companies that hold their client sites hostage). WordPress was the perfect solution in combination with Avia Layout Builder (a drag-and-drop layout builder). It's user-friendly for beginners and has long-term support for updates.
