## Project Overview

Sigler Western Museum (formerly Desert Caballeros Western Museum) was in need of a modern overhaul to their existing website. The existing site served it's purpose for featuring events and artists, but lacked the visual attention that the museum deserved.

For this project, we dared to use some of the more playful colors from their brand standards to ensure featured areas of the website were visually striking. It paid off. The client loved the color scheme, intuitive layout, and mobile responsiveness.

Since they were already familiar with WordPress, we stuck with it. This time, however, we created Advanced Custom Fields repeater content structuring for adding / editing page content rather than the inconsistent nature of their original drag-and-drop builder. This way, any employee with access can log in and create a consistent page layout - keeping the design intact and cohesive.

### The heart and soul of the museum

#half-description
The artists are the heroes of every museum. They are the reason people visit Sigler Western Museum from all over the the world. Each year, the museum hosts its annual Cowgirl Up! event featuring **female** western artists from across the US. They have the opportunity to be featured on the website along with their available variety of artwork.

### Keep 'em coming back

#half-description
Besides artwork, museums often have specific exhibitions and  public or private events. Sigler Wester Museum has a number of these offerings each year, so they wanted to ensure the public could easily access dates, times, and availability of these happenings prominently - without having to dig too deep to find on their site. They are now easily accessible from the home page or respective archive pages.
